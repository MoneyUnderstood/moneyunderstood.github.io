Preview: the checklist

  1. Mark the dates. Federal Benefits Open Season runs November 9 to December 14, 2026. Changes take effect for plan year 2027.
  2. Check your 2027 health premium. The Office of Personnel Management (OPM) reports the average enrollee share of Federal Employees Health Benefits (FEHB) premiums rises 10.9% for 2027, and the Postal Service Health Benefits (PSHB) enrollee share rises 8.2%. Look up your own plan's number.
  3. Re-enroll in your flexible spending account. Federal Flexible Spending Account Program (FSAFEDS) elections do not carry over. You must re-enroll every year.
  4. Adding a family member? Have documents ready. Proof of eligibility is now required when you add a family member to your health coverage.
  5. Review dental and vision. Federal Employees Dental and Vision Insurance Program (FEDVIP) changes are also made during Open Season.
  6. Compare Self Plus One and Self and Family. OPM notes that in some cases Self Plus One costs more than Self and Family, and you may choose either to cover one family member.
  7. Mind the deadline. Enrollment closes at 11:59 p.m. on Monday, December 14, 2026 (Eastern Time for FEDVIP and FSAFEDS).

Source: opm.gov Open Season page and Plan Year 2027 Highlights. General education, not a recommendation.
